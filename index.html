About Me

Hello! I am Guanke (包冠科). I am a results-driven bioinformatician with backgrounds in genomics and scientific software development. After graduating from the University of Edinburgh with a M.Sc. in Bioinformatics, I have gained experience in designing reproducible computational workflows in Python and R, deploying scalable pipelines using Nextflow, and collaborating closely with clinical and computational teams to transform large-scale clinical trial and omics datasets into accessible, actionable resources for translational research. Currently, I am part of the CG-TIC team, where I contribute to the cleaning, harmonisation, and preprocessing of large-scale Electronic Health Records (EHR) data.
