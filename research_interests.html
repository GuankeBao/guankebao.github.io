Research interests

My research interests are in the areas that connect genomics, clinical data, and translational cancer research.

  • Multi-omics integration, quality control, and harmonisation (genomic, transcriptomic, metabolomic, immunopeptidomic, and clinical data).
  • Reproducible sequencing analysis with community workflows (e.g. Nextflow: WGS, WES, RNA-seq, scRNA-seq) on HPC and secure research platforms.
  • Clinical trial data systems, real-time visualisation, and FAIR-style access for interdisciplinary teams (e.g. REDCap, cBioPortal).
  • Gene regulation and transcriptome-scale resources; motif and target prediction from large public compendia.
  • Software engineering for bioinformatics: version control, CI/CD, and DevOps-minded pipelines in regulated and collaborative settings.
